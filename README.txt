A LITTLE SURPRISE FOR YOU — STARTER WEBSITE

FILES
- index.html: webpage structure
- style.css: pastel pink and lavender design
- script.js: welcome page interactions
- assets/photos: put puzzle and personal photos here
- assets/audio: put voice recordings here
- assets/gifts: put gift images here

HOW TO OPEN
1. Extract the ZIP file.
2. Open the MySurpriseWebsite folder.
3. Double-click index.html. It should open in your browser.
4. Click "Start the surprise" to test the welcome page.

NOTE
This is Step 1 starter version. The six activities are shown in the journey overview.
We will add the working 4x4 puzzle, secret question, audio players, letter,
gift capsule selection, and final reveal in the next steps.
Internet is needed for the Google Fonts import; the page otherwise works locally.
